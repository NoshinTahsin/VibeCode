About the kitchen

Comfort food prepared with market-fresh ingredients.

Hearth & Ladle is a fictional neighborhood catering service built around homestyle meals, careful prep, and simple pickup logistics. Menus rotate daily so regular customers can plan weekday lunches, celebrations, and family gatherings without starting from scratch.

Every order is cooked in family-style trays and packed with reheating notes. Dummy content is used for now so the layout and order flow are ready for real dishes later.
